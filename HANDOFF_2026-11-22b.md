THE BLOCKER (unchanged, and T20 did not narrow it)
backboardd drains the queue intact, but the digitizer stream never
reaches an accepted gesture. Three live branches:
(a) the gesture is rejected at the HID layer (event shape/flags),
(b) backboardd's HIDEventSystem drops it before dispatch,
(c) backboardd dispatches but SpringBoard never routes/accepts it.
A negative swipe cannot separate them (claim-0196 K5), which is exactly
why T20's result is branch 2 of the decode ladder and not a verdict.
